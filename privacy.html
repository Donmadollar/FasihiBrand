Privacy Policy

Last updated: 26 October 2026

FasihiBrand respects your privacy. This policy explains what information we collect when you use our website, how we use it, and the choices you have.

Information We Collect

When you submit a story, we collect the details you enter in the form: your name, email address, story title, genre, the story itself, and an optional social media link. We do not collect payment information, and we do not require you to create an account.

How We Use Your Information

  • To review your submission and decide whether to publish it.
  • To contact you about your submission by email.
  • To publish selected stories with your name and social media link, which you provide for that purpose.
  • To keep the platform safe and prevent spam or misuse.

Your email address is never published. It is used only to communicate with you.

Where Your Information Is Stored

Submissions are processed and stored by our hosting provider, Netlify, and delivered to our editorial team. We share your information only with service providers needed to run the website. We do not sell your personal information.

Published Stories

Once a story is published, it is public and can be read, shared and found through search engines. Only submit work you are comfortable sharing publicly.

How Long We Keep Information

We keep submissions for as long as needed to review, publish and manage them. Unpublished submissions may be deleted after a reasonable period.

Your Choices

You may ask us to show you the information we hold about you, correct it, or delete it. You may also ask us to remove a published story at any time. Contact us using the details below and we will respond promptly.

Children

Our website is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect information from them.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The date at the top shows when it was last changed.
