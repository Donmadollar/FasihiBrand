Submission Guidelines

Every submission is reviewed by our editorial team before publication. Please read these guidelines before you submit.

What We Accept

Fiction and short stories, poetry, personal and life stories, biographies, cultural and historical narratives, and essays. Submissions may be in English or Swahili.

Our Standards

  • Original work: submit only your own work, or work you have the rights to share. Plagiarism is never accepted.
  • Quality and clarity: check your spelling, grammar and presentation before sending.
  • Respect for readers: we do not publish content that is abusive, hateful, discriminatory or intended to harass.
  • Honesty: where your story includes real events or people, present them truthfully and obtain permission where needed.

Copyright and Ownership

You keep the copyright to your work. By submitting, you confirm that it is your original work and that you have the right to share it with FasihiBrand. You may ask us to remove your story at any time.

Review and Publication

We assess originality, clarity, literary quality and suitability for the platform. Submitting does not guarantee publication, and we may request revisions. Submission is free, and you will hear from us within 7 to 14 days.
