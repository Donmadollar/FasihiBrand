Growth

The Art of Becoming

by Eudora Ayleen

I have learnt, but too soon, how convicted voices can speak, How the strongest of souls can bend without appearing weak, How hearts build homes where broken pieces meet, And how life's deepest lessons are rarely learned in the streets.

I have learnt how quickly forever can fade, How promises weaken and memories slowly degrade, How fire can comfort, yet leave behind scars, And how some of our greatest lessons come disguised as wars.

I have learnt that thoughts can become louder than truth, That time has little mercy for the innocence of youth, That we sometimes measure our worth by the weight of our fears, And mistake silent suffering for the passing of years.

I have learnt that loss is not always defeat, That broken roads can lead to places we need, That falling does not mean the journey must end, And even the wounded have the strength to mend.

I have learnt to stop begging life to be kind, To stop searching for answers that were never mine to find, For the strength I once waited for the world to reveal, Was growing within me through everything I had to feel.

I have learnt that scars are not stories of shame, That surviving the storm does not leave you the same, That growth is not always a beautiful sight, Sometimes becoming means walking alone through the night.

And now I am learning, though I am not yet complete, That becoming takes courage, patience and defeat, I am still finding my way, still learning to be, Still making peace with the person inside of me.

Perhaps that is the beauty of all we become, Not perfect, not fearless, not distant or numb, But honest enough to accept what we see, Brave enough to change, and humble enough to be.

A soul still unfolding, a heart still becoming, A life still uncertain, yet endlessly blooming. Not a finished story, nor all that I seem, But a beautiful work in progress, Still becoming the dream.
