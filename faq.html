Frequently Asked Questions

For Authors

Is it free to submit a story?

Yes. Submitting to FasihiBrand is free.

How long until I hear back?

Our team reviews every submission and replies by email within 7 to 14 days.

Does submitting guarantee publication?

No. We look at originality, clarity, literary quality and suitability for the platform. We may decline a story or ask for revisions before publishing.

What can I submit?

Fiction and short stories, poetry, personal and life stories, biographies, cultural and historical narratives, and essays, in English or Swahili. Please read our submission guidelines first.

Do I keep the copyright to my work?

Yes. You keep your copyright. By submitting, you allow us to publish your story on this website and promote it on our social media channels.

What will be published about me?

Your author name, bio, achievements, writing categories and social media links appear with your story. Your email address is never published.

Can I remove or correct my story after it is published?

Yes. Email us with the link to your story and tell us what to change or remove. We will act on it promptly.

For Readers

Can I share stories I enjoy?

Yes. Use the share buttons on each story to send a link. Please do not copy or republish a story without the author's permission.

How do I report a problem with a story?

Email us with the link to the story and a short explanation, such as a copyright concern or offensive content. We will review it and remove the content where appropriate.
